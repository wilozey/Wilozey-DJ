# YouTube Channel Structure for Suno Releases

## Channel pillars
1. **Singles**
   - One song per upload
   - Best for testing audience response quickly
2. **Albums / EPs**
   - 8–12 tracks per long-form upload or playlist rollout
   - Ideal for binge listening and watch-time growth
3. **Radio Streams**
   - Genre and mood blocks (1H, 3H, 8H, 24H)
   - Build recurring listener habits

## Playlist taxonomy
- By **Genre**: Afrobeats, Gospel, R&B, Hip-Hop, Pop, Reggae, Dancehall, EDM, Lofi, Jazz, Soul, Country, Cinematic, Worship, Instrumental, Experimental
- By **Mood**: Chill, Uplifting, Focus, Reflective, Party, Worshipful
- By **Use case**: Study, Drive, Prayer, Workout, Background
- By **Release format**: Singles, EPs, Albums, Live Radio Mixes

## Upload cadence suggestion
- **Mon**: Single release (with Shorts teaser)
- **Wed**: Mood playlist refresh
- **Fri**: Album/EP or 1H radio block premiere
- **Weekend**: 3H+ radio block upload and community poll

## Video metadata structure template
- **Title**: `{Song Title} | {Genre} {Mood} Vibes`.
- **Description opening**: `Original {Genre} music by {Project Name}.`
- **Description body**:
  - Theme/story note
  - Credits + rights notice
  - Playlist links
  - CTA for comments/subscription
- **Tags**: include genre, mood, use-case, "original music", "Suno music".
- **Hashtags**: 2–4 max, genre-first.

## Monetization-safe checklist (per upload)
- Confirm paid-plan and commercial use rights for generated audio.
- Confirm no copyrighted samples or artist-name references in prompts/lyrics.
- Confirm visuals are original or properly licensed.
- Confirm description states the music is original and channel-owned.
- Confirm added value exists (curation, visual identity, narrative, branding).

## Radio block design
- Rotate by priority and avoid adjacent repeats.
- Keep sonic arc: warm-up → peak → cooldown.
- Add chapter timestamps every track for long-form videos.
- Keep separate recurring brands:
  - `{Genre} Nights`
  - `{Mood} Sessions`
  - `{Project Name} 24/7 Radio`
